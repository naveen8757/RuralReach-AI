# RuralReach AI — Phase 15

Professor Intelligence Dashboard adds a central analytics view for student mastery, attendance, assignment completion, learning engagement, lecture downloads, classroom quality and rule-based AI intervention recommendations.

Open `professor-analytics.html` after starting the server.

This is a hackathon-ready analytics prototype. AI intervention recommendations are local rule-based insights, not a generative model. Production deployment should add authenticated roles, database-backed event validation, privacy controls and scalable analytics storage.
