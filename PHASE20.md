# RuralReach AI — Phase 20
## AI Career & Skill Pathway

Phase 20 converts the student's existing learning signals into an offline-first career guidance dashboard.

### Features
- Career-track matching for AI & Data Science, Software & Cloud, and Embedded & IoT.
- Uses local Smart Learning mastery and assessment activity.
- Career readiness score.
- Skill-by-skill roadmap with achieved/next/locked states.
- Learning signals explaining why a track is recommended.
- Seven-day action plan.
- Offline persistence through localStorage.
- PWA shortcut and service-worker cache entry.

### Honesty
This is an offline rules-based career guidance engine, not a claim of professional career prediction. Recommendations are educational guidance and should be reviewed with a professor/mentor.
